The `ixo-chain` tools discover their data progressively, and know far more about an account.

- **`chain_lookup` request shape changed.** Each request is now `{ kind, args }`: the kind's fields go inside `args` instead of sitting flat beside `kind` (`{ kind: 'claims', args: { collectionId: '8250' } }`, not `{ kind: 'claims', collectionId: '8250' }`). The tool schema names the kinds only in an enum, so it stays the same size however many kinds exist; each kind validates its own `args`, and a wrong argument refuses only that request (`invalid_input`, pointing to `describe_chain_kinds`) instead of the whole call. Results carry `source` (`chain` live, `blocksync` the index), and a failed Blocksync POST fails only the Blocksync requests.
- **New `describe_chain_kinds { kinds? }`**: without names, the catalogue of every kind (name, source, one-line summary); with up to 8 names, each kind's arguments as JSON schema, an example request and what its result means.
- **New `get_account_overview { address?, sections? }`**: one call for "tell me about my account". Ten sections (balances, fee grants, authz, smart account, account type, staking, liquid staking, claims, governance, DAOs), each from the existing kinds in one lookup call (one Blocksync POST, the 20 s budget): counts, the top 3 rows and `more`, the request for the rest; a failing section or part answers on its own. Parts read shared data once: the DAOs and their open proposals come from one membership read, and with balances also asked the liquid staking holdings come from the balances page. Fee grants given are left out by default (the chain scans every fee grant for them) and named in the section's `notRead`; claims are counted from `hasNextPage`, not a full `totalCount`; `notVoted` leaves out DAO proposals whose voting time is over. Only a complete answer (no failed part, no `…Unavailable` read inside one) is kept 20 s per isolate (at most 200); a call joining one in flight throws only its own turn's abort, and reads again itself after the other turn's abort or a timeout of the other call's budget.
- **New knowledge areas** as `chain_lookup` kinds: fee grants, authz grants (bank, cw20 and cw1155 caps of the ixo claims authorizations), smart-account authenticators, account type, staking (delegations, unbonding, rewards), spendable vs locked balances, liquid staking (each PAY-like token valued as the keeper unstakes it: `valueInIxo` and a 6-decimal `rate` from the pool's net amount and supply; the chain's truncated rates only as `chainStakeRate`/`chainUnstakeRate`), network economics, governance proposals with a voter's vote (`voter`, default the user) and, in voting, `yesShare`, `vetoShare`, `turnout` and `passingNow` by the chain's tally rule, claim intents (read from the account's own key onwards) and collection membership from the chain; token history, evaluations (by `claimId`, `collectionId` or `evaluator`, with bank, cw20 and cw1155 payments and `defaultPayment` when the collection's default applies), member budgets, agent deposits (with `lockEnded`), disputes, DAOs (`addedByGroupAdmin` on cw4 group memberships, `nameIsSelfChosen` on DAO names) and DAO proposals (with `yourVote`, `notVoted`, or `voteUnknown` for proposals older than the index's vote history), and liquid-stake history from Blocksync. A partly failed read keeps what answered, marked (`rewardsUnavailable`, `stateUnavailable`, `tallyUnavailable`, `voteUnavailable`, `contractInfoUnavailable`).
- **Bank coins read the same everywhere** outside the core kinds: `{ denom, symbol, amount, amountBaseUnits, verified, origin?, trace?, metadataUnavailable? }` with `amount` in display units (`upay` reads as PAY with 6 decimals), shown with the oracle's IBC table, so `IXO_CHAIN_IBC_DENOMS` now applies to every chain and indexed kind and the overview, not only `get_account_balances`. A row's one coin is `coin`, several are `coins`; budgets are `spendLimit`/`spent`/`resetAt` and deposits `coin`/`lockEnded` in both the chain and the indexed kinds; an empty amount says what the chain does with it (`"unlimited"`, `"any"`, `"none allowed"`, `"none"`), never `[]`. The core kinds still give `{ denom, amountBaseUnits }`; cw20 and cw1155 amounts stay base or whole units.
- In one call, Blocksync kinds share one GraphQL document, all inside the existing 20 s budget and 12,000-character answer cap (at most 20 distinct denom lookups per request). Every read attempt of a user — chain REST, denom lookups and Blocksync POSTs, across all of that user's calls, `get_account_balances` included — takes one of 5 per-user permits per isolate. Transport: 429, 502, 503, 504 and network errors mark an upstream unhealthy for 10 s; a `Retry-After` over 5 s is not waited for (`rate_limited`, with the wait); no attempt starts with under 1 s of budget, and running out says the tool's time budget ran out; a 400 counts as "none" only for a gRPC code 3 or 5; Blocksync database-pool errors are transient; account arguments verify the bech32 checksum; logs carry error classes, never messages. Fee and authz grants are read without `count_total` and kept 30 s; governance lists and tallies are kept 60 s.
- `get_account_balances` answers as before. The operating guide now opens with "Start with `get_account_overview` for broad account questions; for one fact use `get_account_balances` or `chain_lookup`", the read-only rule again says the Hub applies the account's limits, and the untrusted-text rule covers titles and labels and says the chain is public.
