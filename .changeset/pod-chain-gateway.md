POD Creator now creates a finished POD on chain. The bundled plugin uses a real chain gateway by default: `prepare_pod_transaction` takes the POD's name (and an optional description, at most 600 characters) from the design, checks that the user's identity is on chain and lists their account (the chain refuses the POD otherwise), reads their public key, reserves the POD's public profile on the oracle's homeserver and prepares one `MsgCreateEntity` owned by the user, with the relayer node of the network's Portal (`POD_CREATOR_RELAYER_NODE_DID` overrides it). The summary the user approves quotes the exact name and description. The profile is published only after the user approves, right before their own Portal wallet is asked to sign; if the homeserver is down the approval is kept. `confirm_pod_creation` looks the transaction up on chain for up to 20 seconds, keeping on through brief chain outages, checks that it is the batch this conversation sent and that the POD belongs to the user, and returns the new POD's DID and its Portal link (`POD_CREATOR_PORTAL_URL` overrides the default). When the POD is not on chain yet it says so and can be asked again with the same hash, in the same turn or later. A new tool, `add_pod_domain_card`, then adds the POD's public domain card as the Portal does (one more wallet signature, once per POD) and asks the domain indexer to index it. Expected refusals of every step come back as results the model can explain, not errors. In the design pipeline, a stage passed during a turn now hands over to the next stage's specialists on the very next model call (every specialist is bound once a design exists and the model is shown only the current stage's; a call to another stage's is refused with the next step). Before, the next stage's specialists were missing for the rest of the turn, and a model that called them went on telling the user they did not exist. Within one turn, a gate specialist that failed is not offered again until the user's next message, and the design cannot be restarted once a specialist ran (a model otherwise re-ran failed checks and restarted the design unasked). Each step's result says which stage and specialists come next, and a specialist of a stage already passed no longer overwrites its section. Plugins can now tell `ctx.matrix.botCredentials({ rejected })` that the homeserver refused a token and get fresh credentials. Mainnet still needs `POD_CREATOR_ALLOW_MAINNET=true`. A fork can still pass its own gateway, or `notConfiguredChainGateway` to keep creation off. A POD created here has no governance group yet (the Portal adds one through a contract message the transaction catalog does not support).
