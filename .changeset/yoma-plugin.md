New opt-in `YomaPlugin` (`@ixo/oracle-runtime-workers/plugins/yoma`): Yoma's programmes and jobs as jambo.yoma.world shows them, applying and logging work through each job's own SurveyJS form one question at a time, and whether logged work was approved and paid. Two tools, `yoma_lookup` (1–6 batched requests) and `yoma_form` (one action per call), `on-demand` by default. Reads go through ixo-chain's bounded HTTP policy and never throw. Applications go out as the Auth Hub's application card `ixo.bid.submit.v1` and logged work as its one-tap claim card `ixo.claim.submit.v1` (files uploaded, answers signed, claim saved and submitted on chain as the person), each when the Hub offers it to the account (`@ixo/action-protocol` 0.7.0, Hub protocol level 5). Sending needs the person's yes in a later message than the full read-back, and what is read back, shown on the card and sent is the same: one card row per answer of the sent data, as Auth Hub checks it (hidden answers cleared, nothing cut); a card in flight (`authorized`, `submitted`, `unknown`) is never cleared or sent again; each send has its own request key; a sent claim makes room for the next one on the same job. Files need the runtime release that passes WhatsApp attachments (`ctx.session.channel.attachments`). Not in `BUNDLED_WORKERS_PLUGINS`, so `survey-core` only lands in oracles that import it. ixo-chain's HTTP helpers take a log tag and two more sources (`job-list`, `document`).
